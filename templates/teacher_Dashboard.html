📌 Teacher Guidelines & Instructions

1. Device Requirement

  • Writing pad / pen tablet is mandatory for taking classes.
  • Mobile-only teaching is not allowed.
  • Stable internet connection and proper audio quality must be maintained.

2. Teaching Quality Policy

  • Student feedback will be monitored regularly.
  • Initial low ratings (below 3 stars) may be tolerated up to the first 10 feedbacks.
  • Continuous poor ratings after 10 feedbacks may lead to temporary block or permanent deactivation.
  • Admin will receive notifications regarding repeated poor feedback.

3. Class Conduct

  • Faculty should join classes on time.
  • Proper explanation and doubt clarification are mandatory.
  • Repeated absenteeism or last-minute cancellations are not accepted.
  • Maintain respectful communication with students and parents.

4. Assessment Requirement

  • Monthly assessments/tests must be conducted during the last class session of every month.
  • Faculty should upload marks/performance to admin dashboard.

5. Payment Policy

  • Payment will be credited based on completed teaching hours.
  • Fake attendance or incomplete sessions will not be counted.
  • Payment cycle will be processed weekly/monthly by admin.

6. Content Responsibility

  • Do not share personal tuition contacts or promotional content with students.
  • Class materials should be syllabus-oriented and educational.

7. Demo Performance Monitoring

  • Initial demo classes will be monitored by admin.
  • Continuation depends on teaching quality and student engagement.

8. Camera & Mic Rules

  • Mic clarity is mandatory.
  • Background noise should be avoided.

⚠ Violation of platform policies may result in temporary suspension or permanent removal from the service.
